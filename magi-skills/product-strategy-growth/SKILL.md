# Product Strategy Growth

Use this skill when the answer affects product direction or business viability.

## Workflow

1. Identify the target segment and urgent problem.
2. Separate product promise from internal mechanism.
3. Define the smallest monetizable workflow.
4. Map pricing to usage cost and perceived value.
5. Choose launch metrics that prove the product is working.
6. Prefer testable rollout steps over broad strategy.
